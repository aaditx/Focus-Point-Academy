Focus Point Academy.

Focus Point Academy was founded in 2015 by Mrs. Neeta Srivastava, a highly skilled and professional Montessori trained teacher having passion to educate children imbibing social sense and culture.

Neeta has proven herself and handled many play schools as Center Head before establishing her own Coaching Institute in Pune, a well known education hub.

Focus Point Academy provides English Medium Coaching classes upto 10th Stantard and Internationally certifeid Diploma in Teacher Training ( Montessori and Early Childhood Education and care )
Focus Point Academy is one of the prominent authorised study centre in Pune by International Montessori Teacher Training Institute ( IMTTI )

In span of 6 years, students have passed their academics with flying colors scoring excellent marks.
We conduct all festival celebration and parties for its students across the year keeping a balance between studies and fun for its students. Focus PointAcadey believes in carving a path for students towards success.
